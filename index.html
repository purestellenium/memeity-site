Features

Everything you need to always have the right meme.

Ctrl+Alt+M

A search pane, one shortcut away

Press the shortcut from any app, type, hit Enter. The meme pastes straight into whatever you were using. Keyboard-first, Raycast-style.

executiontext match

Finds the words in your memes

Search by filename or by the text on the image itself. memeity reads it on your device, so nothing gets sent anywhere to be read. Not on Intel Macs yet, but text read on your other computers syncs over.

Laptop memeity in your Google Drive Desktop

Synced through your own Drive

Sign in with Google and you're set up. Your memes live in a memeity folder in your Drive and show up on every computer you sign in on.

Drop or CtrlV

Add memes in a second

Drag and drop or paste images into the pane, and they're added and synced right away.

GIF

GIFs stay animated

Paste a GIF into Discord, Slack or a browser and it keeps moving.

Up to date

Updates itself

New versions install automatically, so you never have to keep up with releases.

Privacy

Your memes are your business.

Only sees its own folder

memeity can't see the rest of your Drive, only the files and folders it created.

No memeity servers

Your memes go straight between your computers and Google Drive.

No telemetry

No analytics, no tracking, no phoning home. I don't need your data, silly!

How memeity uses your Google account

memeity signs in with Google only to sync your memes between your devices through your own Google Drive. It asks for the narrowest Drive permission available, which lets it see only the files and folders memeity creates, never the rest of your Drive.

There's no memeity server: your memes go straight between your devices and Google Drive, and the developer never receives them. Read the full privacy policy.
